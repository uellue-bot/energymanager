# Acceptance examples

One concrete situation each and what should then be observable. Tests and specifications cite the IDs. Priority: must or should.

| ID | Scenario | Priority | When | Then | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | S1 | must | Home Assistant runs with the heat pump, the RCT inverter and battery, the solar forecast and a weather forecast connected | One dashboard shows: outdoor, room, floor supply and hot-water temperature; heat pump energy; solar power; battery state of charge; household load; grid import and export; the solar forecast and the weather forecast for 48 h | [answered] |
| E2 | S1 | must | A source is unreachable for an hour (e.g. the Daikin cloud) | The dashboard and the recorded data show the gap as missing data, never as a flat or invented value | [answered] |
| E9 | S1 | should (later) | A source has been unreachable for an hour | The person is alerted. Alerts are secondary to getting the maths and the integration working (principal, 2026-11-21) | [answered] |
| E3 | S2, S3 | must | The model or the optimizer is run on a recorded test case: recorded measurements and the forecasts recorded at that time | It shows predicted against measured room temperature, floor supply temperature and heat pump energy, with the error; for the optimizer, the outcome is graded against what actually happened. No fixed period | [answered] |
| E4 | S3 | should | A sunny day follows a cloudy one | The plan moves heat pump operation and the vehicles' charging towards the sunny midday, keeps the floor supply at or below 35 °C and the room within the comfort band, and shows the expected change in grid import. Nothing is written to devices | [answered] |
| E6 | S1 | must | A solar forecast and a weather forecast are issued every hour for a week | Every issued forecast is kept with its issue time, so that an hour of production can later be compared with what was forecast 1, 6 and 24 h before it (walk p1-collect-and-view, step 7) | [assumed] |
| E7 | S2 | must | A recorded day is exported as a test case | The test case contains the measurements and the forecasts as issued, in a fixed file format, and loads into Python identically each time, independent of the running Home Assistant (step 10) | [assumed] |
| E8 | S1 | must | A Daikin outage is mocked without touching the unit | The entities behave as in a real cloud outage (the same states), and the recorded data shows the gap (step 12) | [assumed] |
| E5 | S1 | should | Several days of solar production, solar forecast and weather forecast have been recorded | Production is shown against both forecasts, so that one can judge whether the weather forecast predicts production better than the solar forecast | [answered] |
| E10 | S1, S2 | should | The battery runs a calibration (next due 2026-12-18 to 21) | The record holds the inverter's battery state machine (`power_mng.state`) at least every minute, so the calibration's start and end can be read from it and the phase left out of normal behaviour (requirements 2.3, principal 2026-11-22) | [answered] |
| E11 | S1, S2 | should | The inverter's own logs are read | The record also covers the time before the recording started and the gaps in it, at the inverter's log resolution (5 minutes), with each value marked as coming from the inverter's log (principal 2026-11-22) | [answered] |
| E12 | S2 | should | A day has passed | The heat pump's electrical energy for each 2-hour period of that day, for space heating and hot water separately, is recorded, so that the power inferred from the household load can be checked per period (requirements 2.3, issue uellue-bot/energymanager#2) | [answered] |
| E13 | S6 | should | A day has passed | The record holds satellite-derived irradiance for every hour of that day at the site (global horizontal, direct normal, diffuse, and tilted for the roof plane), kept as fetched, so that expected PV power and module temperature can be computed from what the sun actually did (principal 2026-11-23, issue uellue-bot/energymanager#4) | [answered] |
| E14 | S6 | should | The inverter rescans for the maximum power point while the array produces | The record holds the time of the rescan and the inverter's MPP target and correction for the PV input within a minute, so that a rescan is not mistaken for shading (principal 2026-11-23, issue uellue-bot/energymanager#4) | [answered] |
| E15 | S2 | must | A house-and-unit model candidate (track A) has run on today's heating curve and schedules for a week | Its predictions as issued 24 h earlier are shown against the measurements, one plot per pair: room temperature (thermometer), heat pump power draw, the shift or setpoint in effect; with the room-temperature error over the week (first target 0.2 °C) (walk p1-evaluate-before-switchover, step 2) | [answered] |
| E16 | S3 | must | An optimizer candidate (track B) has run in shadow for a week | Its chosen shifts and switching times are shown against the ones that ran, one plot per pair; with the room temperature it predicts under its plan, and its predicted saving against the status quo in grid import and in heat pump electricity (kWh, not €), and the share of time its advice was outside the acceptable band (step 3) | [answered] |
| E17 | S2, S3 | should | Several candidates of a track have run in parallel | All candidates of that track are shown in one plot with overall figures (RMS error; for optimizers, electricity saving), and each candidate's predicted-against-measured pairs can be opened on their own; every view fits without long scrolling and every plot is labelled with candidate, quantity and which line is predicted and which measured (step 5) | [answered] |
| E18 | S3 | should | A week of PV production and forecasts has been recorded | Each forecast source is shown against actual production, one plot per pair, with the error for the next hours (1–6 h) and for tomorrow (18–30 h) separately (step 4) | [answered] |
| E19 | S2 | should | Several weeks of household load have been recorded | The heat pump's compressor starts per day and run lengths are shown; once a house-and-unit model fits, the same weeks are simulated with overshoot [9-04] 2 °C and 4 °C and the difference in heat pump electricity is shown (walk, separate question) | [answered] |
